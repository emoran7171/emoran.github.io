    <h3>Column Mapping the Rosette Molecular Cloud</h3>
    <p>At West Virginia University, my REU research focused on star formation in the Rosette Molecular Cloud (RMC) under the supervision of Professor Jason E. Ybarra. Previous studies of the RMC assumed that its stars formed via triggered star formation, but more recent work has shown that the stars are too close in age for that to be true. To investigate this, I wrote code to extract fluxes at 70, 160, 250, and 350 µm from archival Herschel PACS and SPIRE observations, and performed pixel-by-pixel blackbody fitting at the selected wavelengths to model and map the dust temperature and gas column density within the cloud. Other studies of the RMC’s early stellar evolution have utilized my maps, such as <a href="https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025MNRAS.543.1196V/abstract">Villa-Durango et al. 2025</a>.</p>
